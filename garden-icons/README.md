# Garden App — Starter Icon Pack

Free icons for the garden planning app. Two sets, both free to use in the app
(including a commercial app) as long as you include the credits below.

## 1. OpenMoji — vegetables & flowers (25 icons)
Folders: `openmoji-svg/` (scalable SVG) and `openmoji-png/` (72x72 PNG, ready to drop in).
Tomato, carrot, eggplant, corn, broccoli, potato, cucumber, leafy green, hot pepper,
mushroom, herb, seedling, strawberry, grapes, bell pepper, garlic, onion, rose,
sunflower, tulip, cherry blossom, hibiscus, blossom, four-leaf clover, wilted flower.

## 2. game-icons.net — ground materials & extra plants (12 icons)
Folder: `game-icons/` (single-color black SVG — recolor/tint freely in the app).
Wood pile, stone pile, sand castle (sand), grass, wheat, carrot, broccoli,
cabbage, grapes, leek, acorn, flax.

## Tips
- SVG scales cleanly to any tile size (1 inch and smaller); the 72x72 PNGs are
  ready to use as-is in .NET MAUI / WinUI.
- For sand, wood chips, grass, and rocks as ground cover, consider pairing these
  icons with simple solid-color or textured tiles — a small picture of a rock
  reads worse at 1 inch than a gray tile with the stone-pile icon on it.
- No "pebbles" icon exists in game-icons.net; stone-pile is the closest match.
